The City

Chania is the capital of the Chania region on the island of Crete. The city can be divided in two parts, the old town and the modern city.

You will find the old town surrounding the old Venetian harbor, which was built in the 14th century. The modern city is where most of the locals live and work, filled with vibrant markets, cafes, and historical monuments.
